## Screens shown in the prototype
1. Welcome - "WELCOME TO KIDZO", "Play, learn and create magical memories together.", language line: English, Kinyarwanda, Francais, Kiswahili.
2. Who's playing - profile picker, "Pick your adventure buddy", "Add child", "Family Mode".
3. Kid home - "Find an adventure", "FEATURED ADVENTURE: The Secret Sky Garden", "See all", star counter.
4. Adventure detail - "INTERACTIVE ADVENTURE", 18 min, Ages 5-8, 4.9 rating, skill chips (Problem Solving, Kindness), language chip "EN +3", "Available offline", 186 MB, "Download".
5. Family Mode choice - "Parent's turn!", branching choices ("Catch a sunbeam", "Use the magic mirror").
6. Adventure complete - "+30 stars", "STICKER UNLOCKED: Moonbeam Butterfly", "Your adventure movie is being made!".
7. Calm / Bedtime mode - "Good evening, Mia", "TONIGHT'S STORY: The Cloud Who Couldn't Sleep", 12 min narrated, "Moon songs", "Ocean dreams", "Games are tucked away until tomorrow", 38 of 60 min.
8. Parent Control Center - "Good morning, Amara", child card (Mia, Little Explorer, age 5), TODAY stats (Adventures, Stars earned), Quick actions (Download content, Bedtime mode, Lock app now), THIS WEEK insight.
9. Bedtime settings - "Gentle evenings", preview "Mia's Calm Mode", "Games rest until 7:00 AM", DAILY LIMIT slider 20-90 min, Schedule.

## Design system sections
Color, Typography (Baloo 2 Display ExtraBold 32/40, Nunito Body Semibold 16/24; H1, H2, Body, Caption), Mascot "Meet Zo", Buttons (incl. disabled "Not available"), Content cards, Chips and tags (EN, RW, FR, SW), Feedback and controls (correct "That's right!", retry "Let's try again", download progress, Downloaded/Ready offline, Narration toggle, Volume, "Saved!" toast).
Target frame: iPhone 15, 393 x 852. 8pt grid. Touch targets 64 / 40 / 28. Footer: "React Native, Expo, WCAG AA".
